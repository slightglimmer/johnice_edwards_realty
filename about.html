About Johnice

Hi, I'm Johnice. Welcome home.

I'm a licensed Michigan real estate professional and a homeowner. I created this site because so much of what matters about owning a home, like how equity grows, why taxes change, and which repairs can't wait, is rarely explained in plain language.

My goal is simple: to make real estate knowledge more accessible, so you can make decisions about your home with clarity and confidence, whether you're saving for your first place or planning your next renovation.

What guides everything here

Practical education

Clear explanations you can use, with real steps instead of jargon and hype.

Financial awareness

Understanding the full cost of owning, from taxes and insurance to upkeep and upgrades.

Confidence at home

Feeling good about the place you live, and knowing how to care for it well.

About this site

An education-first resource

Johnice Edwards Realty is a place to learn, not a listings site. You'll find guides, renovation ideas, and homeowner tools here, shared to inform rather than to sell.
